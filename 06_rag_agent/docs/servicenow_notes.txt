The AI Control Tower (AICT) is ServiceNow's internal platform for observing
and governing enterprise AI usage. It provides adoption metrics, cost
tracking, and policy enforcement across AI-powered workflows.

The Configuration Management Database (CMDB) is the system of record for
all IT assets ("configuration items") and the relationships between them.
Most ServiceNow modules, including AICT, read from or write to the CMDB.

A downsell/upsell ACV (Annual Contract Value) model predicts whether a
customer account is likely to reduce or expand its contract value, using
usage, support, and billing signals as features.
